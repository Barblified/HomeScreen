3. Model used

The empirical sample is strictly GW1–7 of 2026/27. Player minutes, xG, xA, clean sheets, saves, bonus, cards and defensive contributions are estimated from current-season element histories. Per-90 xG/xA rates are shrunk toward current-season positional medians with a weak 360-minute pseudo-sample:

posterior rate = (minutes × observed rate + 360 × positional rate) / (minutes + 360)

Fixtures then adjust attacking and clean-sheet components through official FDR and home/away state. The player forecast is 82% component expectation and 18% shrunken current-season realised points, retaining a small correction for omitted scoring channels.

After those means are defined, 20,000 common-random-number simulations jointly draw player availability, shared team shocks and over-dispersed scoring. They apply legal formations, captain/vice fallback and ordered autosubs; ordinary bench points are excluded unless an autosub occurs.

  • Aleatoric: match and scoring randomness inside the simulation.
  • State: injury flags, minutes, manager/system, captain fallback and autosubs.
  • Epistemic: only seven completed Gameweeks and model specification. Shrinkage and walk-forward testing reduce, but do not remove, this uncertainty.
