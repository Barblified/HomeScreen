4. Model definition, theorems and correct limits

Specified generative architecture (not fully executed this run): for each player and match, draw an availability/start state, shared team-attack and defence conditions, minutes conditional on role, goal/assist counts conditional on expected opportunities, clean-sheet and defensive events, bonuses/cards, then map those to official FPL scoring; jointly apply legal XI, captain/vice fallback and autosubs. Separate aleatoric match randomness, epistemic small-sample rate uncertainty and state uncertainty from news, minutes, prices and private squad. Calibrate on completed 2026/27 GWs only, validate on strictly later GWs by walk-forward test. Only then estimate win probabilities and 80% path intervals from common random draws.

Bellman principle of optimality (dynamic programming): Vₜ(s)=maxₐ E[pointsₜ(s,a)−4·hits(a)+Vₜ₊₁(Sₜ₊₁)]. Here s is current squad, budget, free transfers and injury/fixture knowledge; a is any legal action. The key implication is that replacing Fernandes must be compared with keeping Fernandes and retaining next week's second FT, not only this week's player-v-player mean.

Value of information (Jensen's inequality): E[maxₐ U(a,news)] ≥ maxₐ E[U(a,news)] when the choice can be made after receiving relevant news without other costs. Waiting until European matches/press conferences can improve a future decision, but this inequality alone does not prove HOLD wins: its value must outweigh the points or price movement sacrificed today.

Regression to the mean and winner's curse: players selected for extraordinarily high seven-game totals are likely to have enjoyed positive outcome noise. A principled current-season shrinkage prior could use posterior rate r̂=(M·r_obs+κ·r_position)/(M+κ), with M season minutes, κ weak prior-equivalent minutes, and r_position a 2026/27 positional baseline. This is a model specification, not a fitted value or proof of how much Groß regresses. Neither these theorems nor sample xGI imply a particular next-match result.

Walk-forward integrity: prior report cited GW4/5/6 holdout MAEs 1.42/1.32/1.34, but these are prior-run results not reproduced now. An unaudited previous-season prior in another service prevents treating its simulation as a compliant independent holdout today.
