Method actually used

For each player, Gameweek-level points and minutes from GW1–5 were converted to a points-per-90 rate and shrunk toward a weak position-level prior estimated only from 2026/27 regulars. Expected minutes blend the last three Gameweeks with the season mean. Fixture difficulty modifies each event forecast, while explicit availability probabilities model injury/doubt state.

The five-Gameweek forecast is:

xPtsi,g = shrunk P90i × expected minutesi,g/90 × availabilityi,g × fixture multiplieri,g.

The joint Monte Carlo then samples availability separately from an over-dispersed Gamma–Poisson scoring process and applies a shared club shock for within-team covariance. It also applies legal autosubs, captain and vice-captain fallback. Ordinary bench points are not added unless an autosub occurs.
