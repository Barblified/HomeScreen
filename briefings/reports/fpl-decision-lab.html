Method actually used

Only 2026/27 observations were used. Each player's GW1–5 points-per-90 rate was shrunk toward a weak current-season positional prior. Expected minutes blend the last three events with the season mean. Fixture difficulty and explicit availability state then generate player-event means:

xPtsi,g = shrunk P90i × expected minutesi,g/90 × availabilityi,g × fixture multiplieri,g.

The squad optimisation jointly selects a legal XV, each week's legal XI and Points-EV captain; unused bench value receives only an 8% option/autosub weight. The 10,000-draw joint simulation then samples availability separately from an over-dispersed Gamma–Poisson scoring process, applies shared club shocks, ordered legal autosubs and vice-captain fallback. Ordinary bench points are never counted unless autosubbed.
