Model and uncertainty

Forecasts use completed 2026/27 Gameweeks only. Player rates are shrunk toward a position-level current-season prior equivalent to 360 minutes, then conditioned on recent minutes, availability, home advantage, opponent difficulty and scheduled fixtures. The 20,000-draw joint simulation samples availability, shared team shocks and Gamma–Poisson scoring before applying legal formations, captain fallback and autosubs. Ordinary bench points are excluded.

Aleatoric uncertainty: match-to-match scoring variance. Epistemic uncertainty: only five officially finalised Gameweeks and uncertain true player rates. State uncertainty: Friday availability, roles and the unfinalised GW6 API state.

Walk-forward checks: GW4 MAE 1.42, rank correlation 0.647, bias −0.14; GW5 MAE 1.32, correlation 0.687, bias −0.22.
