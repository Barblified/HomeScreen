Model and uncertainty

The event/state model uses only 2026/27 completed events. Player points per 90 are shrunk toward weak current-season position priors equivalent to 360 minutes, then conditioned on recent and season minutes, verified availability, opponent difficulty and scheduled fixtures. The generative model is defined before simulation: player availability, shared team shocks, Gamma–Poisson scoring, legal formations, captain fallback and autosubs. The 20,000-draw path comparison uses common random numbers. Ordinary bench points are excluded.

Aleatoric uncertainty: irreducible match scoring. Epistemic uncertainty: only six completed Gameweeks and winner's-curse risk around early leaders such as Groß and De Cuyper. State uncertainty: unfinished GW7, future team selection and upcoming club news. Walk-forward holdouts remain GW4 MAE 1.42/rank correlation 0.647 and GW5 MAE 1.32/correlation 0.687; the ranking signal is useful but fine differences are not stable.
