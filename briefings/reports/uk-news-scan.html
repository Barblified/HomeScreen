Commons approves Budget resolutions; detailed scrutiny continues

The Commons voting record shows majorities for resolutions including a temporary bank surcharge increase, the VAT treatment of public electric-vehicle charging and a high-value council-tax surcharge. The Budget document says the bank surcharge rises from 3% to 6% from January 2027 to March 2028 and clarifies 20% VAT on public charging from 1 November. Why it matters: The measures affect fiscal distribution, public-charging costs and some high-value properties, but do not constitute an immediate preparedness hazard.

Evidence / uncertainty: Primary parliamentary and Treasury material is corroborated by an independent division record. Implementation details may change through legislation and administration.

UK Security / Infrastructure / Public Safety

CURRENT · 3 NOVEMBER

NCSC says AI is accelerating cyber intrusion and CNI defenders should prepare

The NCSC’s new assessment says AI is making established attack techniques faster, cheaper and easier to scale. It judges AI-enabled malware highly likely to be used in a ransomware attack on UK critical national infrastructure by 2028, while stressing that scenarios are not predictions. The Government also opened a time-limited Cyber Essentials discount for eligible small organisations.

Why it matters: Legacy systems, weak recovery and credential compromise are more practical household and small-business pathways than speculative “super-AI” attacks.

Evidence / uncertainty: This is a forward-looking official assessment, independently reported as mostly amplifying familiar tactics. It is not notice of an active incident.
