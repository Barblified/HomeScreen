Foreign defence contracts over £50m to face UK economic-benefit tests

Why it matters. This changes procurement incentives and may strengthen domestic resilience, but the economic result depends on enforceable agreements and transparent measurement rather than contract value alone.

Evidence and uncertainty. The policy and threshold are official and independently reported. It is too early to quantify jobs or industrial capacity created.

Commercial ship sinks after Black Sea drone strike; attribution remains unresolved

Why it matters. Repeated attacks near NATO members can raise shipping insurance, freight and escalation risk. Any UK household effect is indirect and likely to arrive through prices or supply-chain timing, not immediate shortage.

Evidence and uncertainty. Reuters verified footage and AP independently corroborated the core facts. Analysts discussed possible drone design, but that is not proof of the attacker.
