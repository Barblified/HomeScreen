Independent review launched into Henry Nowak murder and agency response

New official review · 26 October The Home Secretary has commissioned an independent two-phase review led by former Lord Chancellor Alex Chalk KC. Phase 1 will examine what police, health, local-government, education, employer and relevant faith bodies knew during the five years before Henry Nowak’s murder, whether opportunities to identify and manage risk were missed, and how the family was treated by the criminal-justice system. Household risk pathway: No immediate household action. Follow verified findings and recommendations; do not turn disputed background claims into collective blame.

Shop-price inflation accelerates as retailers pass through costs

New data · 27 October, 00:02 GMT · medium confidence The British Retail Consortium’s October sample shows shop prices 2.1% higher than a year earlier, up from 1.4% in September and the fastest rise since February 2024. Food inflation rose to 2.9%; non-food inflation more than doubled to 1.7%. The trade body attributes the pressure to transport, energy, employment and packaging costs. [64][65]

Household risk pathway: Review ordinary food, fuel and energy spending and compare prices; there is no evidence of shortage and no case for stockpiling.
