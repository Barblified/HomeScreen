Flash PMI signals stronger activity and renewed hiring

Updated market reaction · 23 October S&P Global’s flash composite output index rose to 53.1 in October from 52.0 in September, its strongest reading since February and above every forecast in Reuters’ poll. Firms reported growing orders, the first net hiring increase since the 2024 tax rise and the fastest manufacturing-output growth in more than two years. Evidence / uncertainty: The primary flash release and Reuters agree. PMI measures survey responses rather than realised GDP, and exchange rates are volatile.

Household risk pathway: Potentially supportive for jobs and some import costs, but higher input costs and rate expectations can still reach prices and borrowing.
