Budget documents confirm £100 household payment and extended electricity-VAT relief

UPDATED · official documents published 28 October · high confidence The published Budget confirms a £2.6bn Household Energy Payment: £100 paid directly into bank accounts from January 2027 for up to 20 million households in England in council-tax bands A–D, with devolved governments receiving Barnett-formula funding. The temporary electricity-VAT cut is extended to 1 July 2027 and is expected to reduce the April 2027 annual Ofgem cap by about £50. Household risk pathway: No immediate action. Eligible English households can note the January 2027 timetable, but should not change direct debits or budgets until operational guidance confirms payment administration.
