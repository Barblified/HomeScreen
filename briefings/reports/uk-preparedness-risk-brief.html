Correlated risks

Geopolitical tension → cyber/sabotage pressure → local service disruption: the clearest newly strengthened UK cascade; prepare for short continuity gaps, not national collapse.
Saudi–Houthi escalation → aviation/energy infrastructure uncertainty → oil, freight and insurance volatility: explosions near Dhahran are a developing indicator, not confirmed infrastructure damage.
Energy/fuel costs → transport and food costs → weaker household buffers: gradual affordability pressure reduces resilience before any physical shortage occurs.
Coastal flooding → roads/power/telecoms: locally material where tidal alerts coincide with exposed infrastructure.
Seasonal illness → staffing pressure → slower routine services: plausible through autumn, particularly for vulnerable households.
