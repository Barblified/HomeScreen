Budget energy relief — eligibility, timing and funding now confirmed

MODERATE cost pressure · easing signal · high confidence · months The final Budget provides a £2.6bn Household Energy Payment of £100 from January 2027 for up to 20 million English households in council-tax bands A–D, with Barnett funding for devolved governments. Electricity-VAT relief is extended to 1 July 2027 and is expected to lower the April 2027 annual price cap by about £50. The payment is funded by temporarily increasing the Bank Corporation Tax Surcharge from 3% to 6% for 15 months. Gas-standing-charge reform remains a separate Ofgem implementation task. [17][39][18][19][20]

Why it matters: The policy now has defined eligibility, timing and funding, reducing uncertainty for many households, although it is modest relative to wider energy and food-cost exposure.

Evidence / uncertainty: The Budget and HMRC collections are published. Ofgem methodology, operational payment guidance and equivalent devolved schemes remain implementation questions.

Household consequence: No immediate action. Eligible English households can note the January timetable; do not change tariffs or direct debits until operational guidance is issued.
