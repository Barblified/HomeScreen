Bank Rate rises to 4%; another increase is conditional, not preset

Evidence / uncertainty: Primary Bank decision and forecast, independently confirmed by two distinct Reuters reports and Associated Press. The conditional path depends on conflict and energy prices; market pricing can reverse and policy is not preset.
