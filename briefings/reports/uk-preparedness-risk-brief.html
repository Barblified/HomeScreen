MODERATE · RISING PRESSURE · MONTHS–YEARS

NIESR projects a longer inflation overshoot

NIESR’s new outlook projects CPI inflation peaking at 4.4% in February 2027, remaining above the Bank of England’s 2% target through 2029 and returning to target late in 2030; it forecasts 1.6% growth in 2026.

Why it matters: A longer price-level squeeze would prolong pressure on food, energy, borrowing and household contingency budgets.

Evidence / uncertainty: Reuters reported the NIESR release. Exact paths are model-dependent and differ from Bank of England and OBR baselines because assumptions and publication dates differ. This is a forecast, not a realised shock.

Household consequence: Keep ordinary budget headroom, compare tariffs and rotate essentials; do not stockpile in response to a forecast.
