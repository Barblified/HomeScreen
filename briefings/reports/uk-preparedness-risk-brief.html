Cascading interdependencies

  • Storm → power → communications / payments: prolonged outages remove charging, card acceptance and transport information together.
  • Oil → freight → food and bills: duration matters more than one intraday move.
  • Housing constraint → homelessness → health demand: short-term support helps; supply still matters.
  • Cloud concentration → services: no severe broad incident, but offline fallbacks remain prudent.

EDC & household-readiness checklist

  • Phone, charged power bank, tested cables and plug.
  • Torch, spare compatible batteries and battery radio.
  • Paper contacts, medication list and essential numbers.
  • Small cash fallback and two payment methods, stored safely.
  • Warm layer, water and simple food rotated through ordinary use.
  • Vehicle fuel sensibly above empty, seasonal screenwash, blanket and legal roadside kit.
  • Test fire/CO alarms; use cooking and charging equipment only as designed.
