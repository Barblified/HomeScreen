  • Oil → freight → food and bills: duration matters more than one intraday move.
  • Housing constraint → homelessness → health demand: short-term support helps; supply still matters.
  • Cloud concentration → services: no severe broad incident, but offline fallbacks remain prudent.
  • Industrial action → planned care: a November consultant strike could delay non-emergency treatment if negotiations fail.
  • Funding flexibility → maternity variation: removing a ring-fence may widen local differences unless ICB spending and safety outcomes are transparent.
