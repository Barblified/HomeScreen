Compromised case data can make phishing more convincing. Known contact routes and unique credentials break the chain.

Repeated disruption matters more than a single event; track sustained official and market evidence before changing household purchasing.

EDC & Preparedness Checklist

  • Charged phone, small power bank, cable and offline contact list.
  • Hands-free torch/headlamp plus a separate light or compatible spare power source.
  • Potable water stored in clean food-grade containers; rotate and protect from contamination.
  • Warm layer, compact emergency blanket and weather-appropriate footwear.
  • Prescription continuity, basic first aid and smoke/CO alarm checks.
  • Battery radio or another independent way to receive official information.
  • Paper map, essential document copies and modest legal cash contingency.
