Hormuz strikes and ship losses → reduced transit and higher insurance → energy and freight costs: oil eased overnight, but two sinkings and record freight costs keep the disruption pathway active.

Seasonal illness → staffing pressure → slower routine services: plausible through autumn, particularly for vulnerable households.
