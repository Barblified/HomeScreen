Correlated risks

Gulf escalation → oil / freight / aviation → UK inflation and travel disruption. Cyberattack → communications / payments / public services → household access problems. Drought plus local heavy rain → simultaneous water-supply and flood-response pressure. These are plausible cascades, not forecasts that all links will occur.
