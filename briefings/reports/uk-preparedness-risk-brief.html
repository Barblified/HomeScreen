MODERATE · RISING · DAYS–MONTHS

Cold-chain cyber incident demonstrates a practical UK pathway

A handful of UK meat processors and a fresh-food wholesaler had refrigeration controls manipulated. Manual temperature checks prevented reported losses in confirmed cases; the NCSC called national impact minimal and no major supermarket chain was directly affected.

Why it matters: Internet-exposed legacy controllers can turn a digital intrusion into food safety, stock and logistics problems.

Evidence / uncertainty: NCSC statements, specialist trade reporting and mainstream reporting agree on limited UK impact. Attribution and total affected-firm count remain unresolved.

Household consequence: Businesses should patch and preserve manual monitoring. Households need no extra stock beyond normal rotation.

Bank Rate reaches 4% as energy inflation remains the swing factor

The Bank raised its policy rate by 0.25 points on an 8–1 vote and expects inflation around 5% in mid-2027 under its central path. It says policy is not preset and will track energy pass-through, wages and pricing behaviour.

Why it matters: Borrowing and refinancing costs can rise alongside energy and food expenses, producing a correlated household squeeze.

Evidence / uncertainty: The decision is primary evidence and independently corroborated. The path depends heavily on conflict and wholesale prices.

Household consequence: Review variable debt, renewal dates and essential-cost buffers; no abrupt financial action is warranted solely by one meeting.
